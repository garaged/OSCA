# Proposal

M7 implements the next governed milestone after M6 by introducing deterministic F2 event-driven validation contracts and services for order lifecycle, fills, risk outcomes, balanced accounting journals, valuation evidence, projections, reconciliation, and promotion gates.

## Non-goals

- F3 paper accounts or forward automation.
- Runtime live strategy execution.
- Live brokerage or exchange order placement.
- Tick, quote, order-book, or F4 microstructure fidelity.
- ML and LLM lifecycle work.
