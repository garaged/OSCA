# Proposal — M4 research projects, analytics, and visualization

## Why

M3 established temporal correctness for governed market data. M4 adds the governed exploratory research layer that organizes intent, hypotheses, analysis graphs, structured outputs, visualizations, dashboards, and reports with reproducible lineage.

## What changed

- Added project, hypothesis, timeline, ad hoc workspace, and promotion contracts.
- Added analysis graph contracts, validation, and deterministic planning.
- Added structured analytical output contracts with dataset and parameter provenance.
- Added declarative visualization specification, visualization export, dashboard panel, and dashboard contracts.
- Added application services for ad hoc promotion, hypothesis transitions, project timeline ordering, graph planning, evidence-report assembly, and dashboard composition.
- Added traceability, tests, documentation, and retained evidence for M4.

## Impact

Users gain the first governed research lifecycle layer above market data. Exploratory work becomes reproducible and inspectable without prematurely implementing external extension packaging, ML training, backtesting, paper trading, or live execution.
