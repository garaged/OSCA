## Why

M1.1–M1.6 implement the secure walking skeleton, but REQ-0019 and REQ-0020 remain incomplete until version-matched documentation, executable examples, navigation, traceability, and retained operational evidence are reconciled. This bounded change closes M1.7 without redefining accepted product or architecture authority.

## What Changes

- Add one task-oriented M1 run-and-operate guide that routes to existing focused guidance.
- Define and validate representative clean-setup, readiness, diagnostic-job, and recovery documentation examples.
- Consolidate configuration, security, telemetry, troubleshooting, schema, prerequisite, and limitation navigation.
- Reconcile the M1 evidence plan, milestone index, root README, and traceability register.
- Retain M1.7 evidence with exact source and validation identity.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `m1-documentation-operational-evidence`: Version-matched M1 operational guidance, executable-example validation, navigation, and retained evidence.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The accepted M1 specification and existing public contract families remain authoritative.

## Impact

- **Requirements:** REQ-0019 and REQ-0020.
- **Acceptance criteria:** M1-AC-001, M1-AC-003, M1-AC-018, M1-AC-020.
- **Governing architecture:** ADR-0001, ADR-0004, ADR-0005, ADR-0009, ADR-0010.
- **Risk class:** Governed documentation and evidence reconciliation for a high-risk foundation change.
- **Non-goals:** M1.8 exit acceptance, new product behavior, contract changes, architecture redesign, personal-server production certification, or retrofitting completed history.
