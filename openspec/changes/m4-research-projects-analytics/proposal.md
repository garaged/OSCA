# Proposal — M4 research projects, analytics, and visualization

## Why

M3 established temporal correctness for governed market data. The next product outcome is a governed exploratory research project that can organize intent, hypotheses, analysis graphs, structured outputs, visualizations, and reports with reproducible lineage.

## What changes

- Add project, hypothesis, and timeline contracts.
- Add analysis graph contracts and validation.
- Add structured analytical output contracts.
- Add declarative visualization specification contracts and export metadata.
- Add traceability, tests, documentation, and evidence for M4.

## Impact

Users gain the first governed research lifecycle layer above market data. Exploratory work becomes reproducible and inspectable without prematurely implementing external extension packaging, ML training, backtesting, or paper trading.
