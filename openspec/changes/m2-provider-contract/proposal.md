# M2.2 Provider Contract and Fixtures

## Intent

Accept one provider-neutral daily acquisition contract and prove it with deterministic synthetic stock and crypto adapters before any live provider is promoted.

## Authority

REQ-0025–REQ-0029; D-016, D-040; ADR-0003–ADR-0005, ADR-0008, ADR-0014, ADR-0015; accepted provider strategy; RISK-M2-002–RISK-M2-004, RISK-M2-006, RISK-M2-007, RISK-M2-010.

## Exclusions

Live networking, provider SDKs, real credentials, provider-derived redistributed fixtures, routing, canonical persistence, and M2.7 promotion are excluded.
