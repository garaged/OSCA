# P2 Risk Register

| Risk | Description | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| P2-R-001 | Free provider terms may be unclear, mutable, or dataset-specific. | Fail closed; require exact evidence before implementation or promotion. |
| P2-R-002 | A popular unofficial source may appear attractive but violate terms or break unexpectedly. | Exclude unofficial/ambiguous sources from implementation shortlist until compliant official evidence exists. |
| P2-R-003 | Free-tier quotas may be too small for useful workflows. | Classify as low-volume or enrichment-only; require explicit quota evidence in later milestones. |
| P2-R-004 | Macro/fundamental providers may be mistaken for OHLCV market-data substitutes. | Record capability fit and non-fit in the catalog. |
| P2-R-005 | Discovery docs may become stale as provider terms change. | Treat P2 as discovery baseline only; require fresh evidence before adapter implementation or promotion. |
