# M11 Risk Register

| Risk | Treatment | Status |
|---|---|---|
| M11-R-001 Opaque analytical packs | Require methodology, assumptions, limitations, documentation, and fail-closed validation. | Treated |
| M11-R-002 Evidence collapse | Preserve supporting and contradicting evidence references in result and synthesis records. | Treated |
| M11-R-003 Method preference misuse | Require preferred results to be part of the compared set. | Treated |
| M11-R-004 Outcome learning drift | Preserve expected versus realized outcomes and calibration status. | Treated |
| M11-R-005 Portfolio analysis mistaken for order authority | Keep scenario reports as evidence only and defer execution. | Treated |
| M11-R-006 Inaccessible visualizations | Require accessible summaries and export metadata in visualization pack specs. | Treated |
