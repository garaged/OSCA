# D4 Intent — Asset Catalog, Market Browser, and Watchlists

## Outcome
Users can discover, register, organize, and inspect canonical assets without confusing symbols, venues, asset classes, or provider aliases.

## Scope
Canonical search, asset registration, aliases, ambiguity resolution, watchlists, recent assets, asset metadata, data-availability summaries, and asset-detail navigation.

## Non-goals
Recommendation ranking, portfolio ownership, order entry, or provider-specific symbol leakage into user workflows.

## Dependencies
D3 governed data-source and acquisition UX.

## Risks
Ticker collisions, stale metadata, cross-provider identity mismatches, unsupported corporate actions, and misleading availability indicators.

## Exit intent
Ambiguous identities fail visibly; watchlists preserve canonical identifiers; asset detail shows provenance and coverage; offline/catalog behavior is tested; accessibility and large-catalog performance meet declared budgets.
