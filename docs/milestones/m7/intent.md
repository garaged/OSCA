# M7 Intent - F2 Event-Driven Validation Foundation

- **Status:** Active
- **Baseline:** M6 complete
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-25

## Intent

Enable OSCA to validate strategies through deterministic, authoritative, bar-based F2 simulation semantics using typed event streams, order lifecycle evidence, simulated fills, risk outcomes, balanced accounting journals, valuations, reconciliation, rebuildable projections, and promotion gates.

## Outcome

A local owner or automation client can turn M6 strategy decisions and simulated order intents into governed F2 validation evidence without creating live orders, independent paper accounts, or forward paper automation.

## Non-goals

- F3 forward paper evaluation or independent paper accounts.
- Durable market-aware schedules.
- Live brokerage or exchange execution.
- Tick, quote, or order-book fidelity.
- ML experiment lifecycle.
- LLM orchestration.
- Provider production promotion.
