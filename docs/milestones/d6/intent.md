# D6 Intent — Research Projects, Saved Workspaces, and Integrated Evidence

## Outcome
Users can organize a research question, assets, data revisions, charts, strategies, experiments, notes, and evidence into a reproducible project.

## Scope
Project lifecycle, saved workspaces, pinned evidence, research timeline, notes, lineage navigation, cloning, archiving, and governed export.

## Non-goals
Collaborative multi-user editing, recommendation authority, or arbitrary executable notebooks inside the desktop shell.

## Dependencies
D5 analysis workbench and existing evidence contracts.

## Risks
Hidden mutable references, oversized projects, broken links after migration, and unclear distinction between notes and authoritative results.

## Exit intent
Projects reopen deterministically, preserve immutable references, distinguish user notes from generated or calculated evidence, support recovery and export, and remain compatible with CLI-generated evidence.
