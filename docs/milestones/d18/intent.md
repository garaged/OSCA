# D18 Intent — Accessibility, Localization, Reliability, Performance, and UX Completion

## Outcome
The complete desktop product is coherent, accessible, localizable, resilient, performant, and understandable across supported platforms, including failure, recovery, and degraded-capability states.

## Scope
Cross-product accessibility remediation, pseudo-localization and RTL readiness, reliability hardening, performance budgets, cancellation and recovery consistency, visual and interaction polish, terminology reconciliation, support diagnostics, and documentation completion.

## Non-goals
Major new product areas, weakened gates to meet release timing, or cosmetic polish that hides unresolved correctness or safety defects.

## Dependencies
All user-facing D2-D17 capabilities.

## Risks
Late discovery of architectural accessibility issues, inconsistent state models, accumulated documentation drift, platform regressions, and performance fixes that alter authoritative results.

## Exit intent
Release-blocking accessibility, localization, reliability, security, and performance matrices pass; clean-profile and migration journeys are evidenced on every platform; terminology and help are consistent; no critical or high-severity unresolved release risk remains.
