# D15 Intent — Reports, Sharing, and Governed Export

## Outcome
Users can create portable reports and evidence bundles whose numbers, charts, assumptions, generated explanations, provenance, and limitations remain inspectable outside the interactive workspace.

## Scope
PDF, HTML, image, tabular, and evidence-bundle exports; templates; deterministic source snapshots; provenance; redaction; AI-content labels; export policy; previews; and reproducibility metadata.

## Non-goals
Public publishing service, collaborative hosting, removal of provider restrictions, or exports that imply investment advice.

## Dependencies
D5 visualization, D6 projects, and D12 recommendations.

## Risks
Stale snapshots, inaccessible documents, provider-license violations, sensitive-data leakage, and mismatch between interactive and exported values.

## Exit intent
Exports reference immutable evidence and declared revisions; restricted content is blocked or transformed according to policy; generated prose is labeled; accessible alternatives are present; golden tests verify values and provenance; exported material preserves research-only and simulation boundaries.
